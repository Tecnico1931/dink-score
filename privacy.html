Privacy note

Dink Score is a pickleball scoreboard for friends and clubs. There are no accounts: a club is opened with a short club code. This note says what is stored, who handles it, and how long it stays.

Draft for review. This text was written by the project team to describe what the app does today. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. The site owner should review it before relying on it.

Dink Score does not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, locations or payment details, and does not use advertising or cross-site tracking. Do not put sensitive personal details in names, club names or team names.

Who handles the data (processors)

  • Vercel hosts the website and its server functions, and runs Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. Vercel sees ordinary web request data such as IP address and browser type.
  • Neon hosts the Postgres database that holds the club data above.
  • DUPR, only if the DUPR feature is switched on and a player chooses to link: you sign in on DUPR's own page inside the app, and DUPR's own privacy terms apply to what you do there. Results are sent to DUPR only when someone sends them.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on the phone), only when you turn notifications on: the Dink Score server sends each message, encrypted so only your phone can read it, through that service, which sees that a message was sent to your phone and its size and time.
  • Google, only when an organizer imports players from a Google Sheet link: the server fetches that sheet once to read it. The fetch is made by the server, not your phone, and only for the link the organizer gives.
  • Team logo websites: if an event uses team logo links, your browser loads those images straight from the websites that host them, which can see your IP address. Public bracket pages show logos only if the organizer has allowed it.

Fonts are served from Dink Score itself; the page does not contact Google Fonts.

How long data is kept

  • Club data is kept while the club exists. There is not yet an automatic expiry for inactive clubs.
  • Failed-attempt network addresses are cleared after about a day (the cleanup runs as the app is used, so a few may linger a little longer).
  • Deleted games and ended shared bracket or result links stop being shown immediately (a result page may stay in a cache for up to 20 seconds); backups kept by the database provider may hold older copies for a limited time.
  • Pickup sessions. A pickup session holds the names people type in and the games they add, under its own 8-character code and with no link to any club. It is refused and deleted 24 hours after it starts (the removal runs as the app is used, and an expired session is refused at once), or sooner when the phone that started it ends it. It reaches a club only if a club member saves its games there, which adds the players' names to that club.
  • A club an organizer deletes stays usable for 24 hours, so the deletion can be undone. After that its games, players, events, leagues and ladders, bracket links, result links, live games and the court board are removed from the database (the removal runs as the app is used, and an expired club is refused at once). Backups kept by the database provider may hold older copies for a limited time.

Young players

Juniors may play, but only with a responsible adult: a parent, guardian or the club manager creates and runs the club and decides what is entered. Dink Score does not knowingly collect data from children who set up a club on their own, and does not ask for any contact details. For junior players we recommend using first names or initials and the emoji avatar rather than a photo. Showing initials in place of names and photos on public pages for juniors is planned as a later feature, not something the app does today. If you are a parent and want a child's name, photo or results removed, contact us below.

Your choices

These are built into the app (club menu, Your data):

  • Download my data (any player). On the phone that picked your name in the club, you can download a JSON file with your saved player (name, picture, self-reported rating, DUPR link if any), the games you played in, with their rally logs, and the events, leagues and ladders you are in. Only that phone can do this, because the phone link is how Dink Score knows the name is yours.
  • Anonymise me (any player; an organizer can do it for a name too). Your name is replaced by a random label such as "Former player 4K7QX" in every game, event, live game, court board and history entry (who changed or confirmed what) of that club. Your picture, DUPR ID and link, self-reported rating, the link from this phone and any account link to the name are removed. The games stay, so other people's ratings and records do not change; they just show the label. This cannot be undone, and anyone who remembers who you were can still guess from the games. Only the club you do it in is changed.
  • Download the whole club (organizers). A JSON file with the club's players and their roles, games (with rally logs), events, leagues and ladders, and the history of member and game changes, or a CSV file of its games. It does not contain the PIN, phone tokens, bracket link tokens or DUPR links and IDs.
  • Delete the club (the creating phone or the organizer PIN). Co-organizers can download the club but cannot delete it. The organizer types the club's name to confirm. Everyone sees the club is scheduled for deletion; the creating phone or PIN holder can undo it for 24 hours, after which the club and everything in it is removed.
  • Delete my account (optional accounts). Removes the account, its passkeys, sign-in sessions and its links to club names. Clubs, games and names stay; use anonymise to change those.
  • Organizers can also delete games and remove saved players in the app.

Not covered by these tools: copies in the database provider's backups, and data already on other people's phones or in files they downloaded. To correct something, ask an organizer to edit the game, or contact us below.
