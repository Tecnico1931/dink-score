The service

Dink Score is a free scoreboard, rating and event tool for pickleball. It is provided as it is, without promises that it will always be available, error-free or keep your data forever. Ratings, forecasts and rankings are for fun and are not official ratings. Dink Rating is the app's own number and is not affiliated with DUPR or any governing body. Back up anything you cannot afford to lose.

Clubs and codes

  • A club code works like an invite. Anyone who has it can see the club and add games, so share it only with people you want in the club.
  • The person or phone that creates a club is its organizer. Organizers are responsible for what is entered in it, and for removing entries on request.
  • An organizer PIN adds protection for deleting games and running events. Keep it safe: it cannot be recovered by the project team.

Your content and conduct

  • You are responsible for what you enter. Only add names, photos and logos you have the right to use, and only for people who are happy to be listed.
  • Do not use the service to harass, impersonate or expose anyone, to attack or overload it, to guess club codes or PINs, or to upload anything unlawful.
  • We may remove content or clubs that break these terms or harm others.

Young players

Under-18s may play only with a responsible adult (a parent, guardian or club manager) who sets up and manages the club and agrees to these terms on their behalf. Please keep junior entries to first names or initials, and do not upload photos of children without a parent or guardian's permission. See the privacy note.
